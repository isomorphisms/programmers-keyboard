Composition and piping notation — working note
==============================================

Keep value piping distinct from function composition.

|>  VALUE PIPE
    A value is sent into the next operation.

↜   MACHINE PIPE LEFT / EDRIÇ <~
    U+219C LEFTWARDS WAVE ARROW.
    A Process on the left consumes the output of the Machine on the right.
    Edriç accepts ↜ as the source spelling of <~ while retaining ASCII <~.

↝   MACHINE PIPE RIGHT / EDRIÇ ~>
    U+219D RIGHTWARDS WAVE ARROW.
    The same machine-pipeline operation with its arguments flipped.
    Edriç accepts ↝ as the source spelling of ~> while retaining ASCII ~>.

>>  THEN / POST-COMPOSE
    Read left-to-right: f >> g means do f, then g.
    A keycap could put THEN above >>.

<<  BEFORE / PRE-COMPOSE CANDIDATE
    If >> is retained for post-composition, << is the natural symmetric candidate
    for the opposite direction. Do not add it as a settled key yet.

∘   ORDINARY COMPOSITION
    Keep the ring operator available for conventional mathematical composition.
    A keycap word such as BEFORE or FIRST may make its reading direction clearer,
    but that label is not settled yet.

The visual model to optimize for is ordinary left-to-right flow:

    x  --->  f  --->  g  --->  h

The notation should make that path obvious rather than force the reader to remember
which side is called left/right composition or multiplication. This matters even
more when f, g, h represent maps or homotopies attached to a loop x on a surface S.

Do not collapse |>, ↜/↝, and >> merely because they describe flow: |> pipes a value;
↜/↝ connect Machines and Processes; >> composes functions.
