## Physical slice and source intent

[pcb/movement-prototype.tsx](https://github.com/isomorphisms/programmers-keyboard/blob/e37ceffaf2e4e23ff60eda25e5eecbd211942e5e/pcb/movement-prototype.tsx) declares a 132 × 70 mm, 12-key 3 × 4 Movement prototype with 19.05 mm pitch. The RP2040 support circuit comes from @tscircuit/common. Rows map to GPIO0–2, columns to GPIO3–6. The intended path is column → switch pin 2 → switch pin 1 → diode anode → cathode → row.

Custom bottom socket pads are 2.9 × 2.5 mm at x offsets ±6.725 mm and opposing y offsets ±1.27 mm. Declared holes include 3 mm socket holes, 4.2 mm shaft clearance and 3.2 mm mounts at (±62, ±31) mm. These are source dimensions, not verified mating tolerances. Exact socket, switch and matrix-diode MPNs are unresolved. Historical MX assumptions do not settle the quiet/low-profile preference.

Circuit JSON contains 63 source components, 146 source traces, 125 PCB traces, 84 vias, 203 SMT pads, 42 PCB holes, 4 plated holes, and 207 source/PCB ports each. There are zero error elements and 89 warning elements: 12 reference-name conventions, 43 unnamed traces, 2 missing-pin traces (USB SBU A8/B8), 1 part-not-found warning (L_AVDD, supplier C1002), 22 supplier-footprint mismatches, 3 underspecified-pin, 3 no-power-pin and 3 no-ground-pin warnings. Warnings require classification; their presence or absence is not an electrical verdict.

## Two urgent contradictions

**Assembly identity:** flat reference D1 names both the MCU LED (source_component_19, XL_1608SURC_06, supplier C965799, top side at −39, 4.2 mm, 90°) and the matrix diode (source_component_40, bottom side at −8.575, 25.55 mm, 0°, no exact MPN). The source IDs differ, but BOM and placement both duplicate D1. This is a confirmed flat-identity defect. [A7](https://github.com/isomorphisms/programmers-keyboard/issues/24) must preserve scoped identity and reject ambiguous assembly joins.

**Connectivity:** the readable netlist shows GPIO0–6 as EMPTY NET PINS while its component table labels ROW0–COL3. Circuit JSON has board ROW0–COL3 nets source_net_0–6 in subcircuit_source_group_13 and same-named MCU-local nets source_net_12–18 in subcircuit_source_group_0. GPIO source_port_41–47 each connects through source_trace_29–35 only to its corresponding local net. No serialized pcb_trace record mentions pcb_port_41–47. This is strong evidence of a scope/connection problem, not yet geometric proof that copper is physically disconnected. [A3](https://github.com/isomorphisms/programmers-keyboard/issues/23) must resolve intent versus exported identities; [A4](https://github.com/isomorphisms/programmers-keyboard/issues/34) must independently test copper.

## Firmware and historical CI

Movement drives one row low, floats the others, enables column pullups, waits a nominal 2 µs for settling, and uses five consistent samples with nominal 1 ms loop pacing. Its 16-slot ring reserves a sentinel (15 usable events) and silently drops when full. Only press events enqueue macros. Report progress checks TinyUSB readiness and send success; lifecycle recovery still needs tests. The inspected semantic macros remain Esc, i, k, j, 0, Shift+4, w, Shift+w, 7, 13, x and dw. CMake selects pico. No semantic remapping was made.

Other keypad samples assume a hypothetical 4 × 4 matrix, contain F13-style tokens and Linux Unicode composition, and distinguish ⇒ from →. Their regex verifier is existing Python migration debt, not permission for new Python glue. Phone controls are a separate Consumer HID experiment. Prototype descriptors use 0xCAFE:0x4004 and a 5 ms polling interval. Host text, Android behavior and physical USB operation have not been demonstrated here.
